Reputation & Reviews

A one-star review sits unanswered. Your next customer sees it.

Whether someone is choosing a hotel, a dentist or a contractor, your reply shows how you handle a problem. AyudaGrow helps you ask for honest reviews, spot concerns early and give customers a reason to trust you.

Request a listing check

How it works

  1. Connect your listings

    Start with your Google Business Profile and Facebook page. On Scale, add every eligible review source relevant to your business.

  2. We check each connection

    We match the listing to the right business and location, check that reviews arrive, and test where an alert goes.

  3. Catch reviews that need attention

    New reviews from connected sites land in one review inbox. Once a source passes testing, concerning feedback can alert the manager you choose.

  4. Reply and learn what to fix

    Reply yourself, approve an AI draft or automate replies according to your plan. Core and Build add a monthly health summary. Scale adds trends by source and follow-up actions.

Getting more reviews

Ask while the visit is still fresh.

We invite every customer to give honest feedback, whatever their experience. Everyone gets the same opportunity to leave a public review on sites that welcome requests.

After a visit, stay or job
Send a review link in a thank-you email or through WhatsApp where enabled. We respect each customer’s contact preferences.
At the counter or on a receipt
A QR code takes customers straight to an eligible review page. No reward or minimum rating is attached.
On your website, from Core
Display customer reviews with a review widget. Invite customers to record optional video testimonials and get their permission before sharing them.
