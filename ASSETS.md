- Poppins font files are included under the SIL Open Font License. The license is at `dist/assets/fonts/OFL.txt`.
- Third-party product names and marks identify compatible services, not endorsements. Their respective rights remain with their owners.
- The WhatsApp Business app icon is an unmodified asset from the official WhatsApp LLC Google Play listing: https://play.google.com/store/apps/details?id=com.whatsapp.w4b . The refreshed icon is described at https://whatsappbusiness.com/blog/whatsapp-business-app-gets-a-new-icon/ .
- Dependency license metadata is retained in `package-lock.json`; installed packages supply their own licenses. Review third-party brand and asset rights for the intended production use.
